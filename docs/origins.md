# The origin ledger

This collection begins with surviving material from Ruslan Aleksejevs’s old repositories. It reconstructs selected ideas, not a complete transcript of his education.

| Historical source | What survives here | Modern chapter |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| coursework archive / Jordan.zip; Numerical archive / Jordan, Jordan_threads, Jordan_MPI | Column operations; “первый шаг ручками”; cyclic MPI column ownership; serial/pthreads/MPI lineage | [Numerical methods](../projects/numerical-methods/README.md) |
| learning archive / linear models and feature engineering | A handwritten classifier, a penalty-gradient mismatch, PCA experiments | [Machine learning](../projects/machine-learning/README.md) |
| music archive / GRU_music_v3.ipynb | The team’s school project and the idea of recurrent MIDI continuation; no historical code copied | [Music](../projects/music-generation/README.md) |
| algorithms archive / Biweekly 99, 100 and Kadane | Selected solutions and comments documenting the reasoning process | [Algorithms](../projects/algorithms/README.md) |
| coursework archive / 1DPlot.zip, 2DPlot.zip, Anime.zip | Oscillation, a saddle surface, and an animation idea | [Visualizations](../projects/visualizations/README.md) |

The coursework archive history reaches March 2019; plotting uploads appear in May and Gaussian elimination in November. The music project was our team’s final project at the Krasnovidovo school in August 2022 and was uploaded to its repository in early 2023. The numerical repository also has January 2023 commits. Upload dates and project dates are kept distinct in the [historical timeline](timeline.md).

The [original-code gallery](../originals/README.md) preserves six substantial historical selections beside links to their modern counterparts. Historical chapter pages retain smaller excerpts; music preserves context without copying its old code. The [machine-readable manifest](source-manifest.json) stores hashes of the full source file and the selected text. Four sources are pinned to audited commits; the coursework archive ZIP is identified by its snapshot date and SHA-256 because this edition does not establish its exact snapshot commit. Private repository names and URLs are omitted; the excerpts and fingerprints stand on their own.

The [marginalia](marginalia.md) preserve selected original comments, including their spelling and occasional profanity. A [personal recollection](stackoverflow.md) preserves a little of the feeling of learning MPI. The 2026 implementations and experiments are identified as new work.

The historical provenance supports “this work grew out of these files.” It does not support “every line and result in this new edition existed then.”
