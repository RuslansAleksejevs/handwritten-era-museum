# Where these projects came from

The common setting is my time studying at MSU's Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics. The dates below anchor particular episodes; an upload date is not necessarily the date the work began.

| When | Setting | Exhibit |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| May 2019 | Early plotting exercises; uploads in the `coursework archive` history | [Pictures of mathematics](../projects/visualizations/README.md) |
| November 2019 | Gaussian-elimination coursework; upload on 22 November | [Numerical methods](../projects/numerical-methods/README.md) |
| 24–31 August 2022 | Krasnovidovo: MSU school “Machine Learning, Neural Networks and Program Verification”; neural-networks track | [Music generation](../projects/music-generation/README.md) |
| Coursework and school notebooks, including August 2022 | `NN4science`, “Introduction to Deep Learning,” and “Practical ML”; a shelf of methods to reuse | [Learning from data](../projects/machine-learning/README.md) |
| 4 and 18 March 2023 | LeetCode Biweekly Contests 99 and 100; solving, revisiting, and commenting on algorithms | [Thinking in algorithms](../projects/algorithms/README.md) |
| 2026 | Revisiting selected ideas with AI, new implementations, explanations, and experiments | [The current collection](../README.md) |

## Historical anchors

- **Krasnovidovo:** [MSU's announcement, published 30 June 2022](https://math.msu.ru/node/1837) gives the dates, place, and two tracks. It points to the [school community](https://vk.com/mm_ml_school_2022) for updates. My archived CV records August 2022 and the neural-networks track; my recollection identifies music generation as our team’s final project at this school. The later January–March 2023 repository history records uploads and edits.
- **Contests:** [Biweekly 99](https://leetcode.com/contest/biweekly-contest-99/) and [Biweekly 100](https://leetcode.com/contest/biweekly-contest-100/). Their dates are also recorded in [a contemporary contest log](https://github.com/dimaglushkov/solutions). My notebooks preserve the solutions and commentary that led to this chapter.
- **2019 coursework:** the coursework archive history, inspected for the early uploads. The original source remains private; selected mathematical code is included in the corresponding chapters.
- **ML:** the original `learning archive` README describes the courses and school material. [Course sources](courses.md) distinguish the surviving notebooks, recovered public materials, and connections still being checked.
