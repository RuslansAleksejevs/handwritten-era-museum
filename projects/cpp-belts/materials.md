# The source shelf

**The Art of Modern C++ Development** was a Yandex/MIPT specialization on Coursera: white, yellow, red, brown and black belts. The [2017 launch article by course author Ilya Shishkov](https://habr.com/ru/companies/yandex/articles/332556/) explains its origin. Mikhail Matrosov co-authored the brown and black belts; the specialization was a team effort. [Author profile](https://cppconf.ru/en/archive/2023/persons/c7d85f1169434a8fa1b3d687feca8e05/).

## Why the old Coursera links disappeared

On **4 March 2022**, Coursera announced the suspension of content from Russian university and industry partners and stopped new enrolments in that content. The specialization's original URL returned 404 when checked on **5 October 2026**. These facts support the account that the courses lost their original distribution channel; they do not imply that every copy disappeared from the internet. [Coursera's announcement](https://blog.coursera.org/coursera-response-to-the-humanitarian-crisis-in-ukraine/) · [Original specialization URL](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/c-plus-plus-modern-development).
